README - AS THEY SAW IT 

MID SEM MILESTONE 

CONTROLS
--------
You will need a mouse and a keyboard! This is a single player game so feel
free to read and click at your own pace :))

- Mouse click: advance dialogue (▼), select choices, and click links.
- Keyboard: a few scenes ask you to type — press any key to reveal a
  character's spoken line (you're not typing your own words; your keystrokes
  drive a reveal animation). Tab/Enter are used to cycle and confirm options
  in a small number of scenes.
- Spacebar: also advances dialogue wherever a plain ▼ continue prompt shows.

WHAT THIS BUILD IS
-------------------
This game in its entirety is a demo — a little vertical slice of what As
They Saw It will be. More specifically, you will play through as Anesu for a
whole week, as Erin's week is still being edited :))

CONTENT WARNING
----------------
This game contains themes of addiction, alcoholism, and recovery, as well as
mental health issues such as depression and anxiety.

HOW TO PLAY
-----------
- Click the ▼ arrow (or press spacebar) to advance dialogue.
- Underlined text is clickable — this is how you make choices.
- Dotted-underlined text marked "(nudge)" can be clicked to reveal a deeper,
  more honest version of what a character is really thinking. You don't have
  to click these — choosing not to affects the story too.
- When a text-entry moment appears, just press any key — you're not typing
  real words, your keypresses reveal a scripted line.
- Watch the top-right corner for the in-game clock and date, and the sidebar
  Memory Bank link (a small red dot means there's something new to read).
